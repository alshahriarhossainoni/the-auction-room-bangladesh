What is The Auction Room Bangladesh?
TAR Bangladesh is a structured trading education platform focused on Auction Market Theory, Market Profile, Order Flow and Options Context for Bangla-speaking traders.
Is the free AMT course really free?
Yes. Our free AMT course series is designed to help traders build a professional foundation before deciding whether advanced mentorship is suitable for them.
What will I learn in the Premium Batch?
The Premium Batch covers Market Profile, Auction Market Theory, Footprint and Order Flow, DOM and liquidity, CVD and delta, options flow and gamma, execution models, risk management, psychology and live market analysis.
Do you provide live market analysis?
Premium members may receive structured live market sessions, educational trade reviews, Q&A support and market breakdowns based on the program schedule.
Does TAR Bangladesh guarantee trading profit?
No. Trading involves substantial risk and no educational program, mentor or strategy can guarantee profits. TAR Bangladesh focuses on education, process, risk management and professional market understanding.
How does Premium access work?
Premium access is account-based. Approved users can receive Premium status through the member dashboard and access content according to their assigned permissions.
How does Copy Trading access work?
Copy Trading access is managed separately from educational access. Eligible members can view their copy-trading access status from the member dashboard.
