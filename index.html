Historical Trade Data

Demo data loaded. Import your own licensed GC time-and-sales data to replace it.

Accepted CSV headers: timestamp,price,size,side. Timestamp must be ISO-8601 with timezone (example: 2026-10-08T13:30:00Z). Side must be BUY or SELL (aggressor side), not position direction. Price must be in GC futures points, size in contracts.

Important: Without a trustworthy aggressor-side field, true bid/ask footprint and CVD cannot be computed. A broker's account trade history CSV is not market-wide time-and-sales data. Depth heatmaps require separate order-book updates.
