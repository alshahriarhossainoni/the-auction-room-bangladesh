Options Flow

Gamma exposure, delta exposure and positioning context

Overview

Options positioning can influence hedging flows and market behavior, but public estimates are model-dependent and should not be treated as deterministic forecasts.

Gamma exposure (GEX) approximates how option-related delta hedging sensitivity changes as the underlying price moves. Delta exposure (DEX) estimates directional delta exposure under a specified model.

Positive and negative gamma regimes

A positive dealer-gamma estimate is often associated with stabilizing hedging under simplified assumptions, while negative gamma can imply reinforcing hedging. Actual behavior depends on dealer positioning assumptions, liquidity and market conditions.

Reading options flow responsibly

Check instrument mapping, expiration, data timestamp and model assumptions. Gold ETF options and COMEX gold futures are related but distinct markets; one cannot substitute mechanically for the other.
