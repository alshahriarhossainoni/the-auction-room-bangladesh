What is order flow trading?

A market order to buy generally executes against available offers; a market order to sell generally executes against bids. Footprint and time-and-sales tools can classify these executions as buyer- or seller-initiated. A large number of aggressive buyers does not guarantee a rally: passive sellers may absorb that demand.

The four observations to make

First identify the auction location: value area, prior high/low, VWAP or a high-volume node. Second assess aggressive participation using traded volume and delta. Third compare effort with result: did price travel or stall? Fourth wait for acceptance, rejection or a retest before defining a trade hypothesis.

Delta versus cumulative delta

Bar delta is aggressive buy volume minus aggressive sell volume during one bar. Cumulative delta adds successive bar deltas. A divergence between price and cumulative delta can indicate changing participation, but it can also result from contract roll, session boundaries, feed quality or absorption; it is not a standalone entry rule.

Absorption and exhaustion

Absorption occurs when substantial aggressive execution is met by opposing passive liquidity with relatively little price progress. Exhaustion is a decline in aggressive activity near the end of an auction. Neither proves reversal. Look for subsequent rejection, a failed retest and risk-defined invalidation.

Example: gold futures near value area low

Suppose GC trades below a prior value area low, aggressive sellers hit bids, yet price repeatedly returns inside value. This may indicate failed downside acceptance. A trader could monitor the reclaim and retest of value; invalidation remains below the rejected extreme. This is an illustrative scenario, not a historical trade or forecast.

What a proper course should cover

A structured curriculum covers auction mechanics, bid/ask execution, footprint reading, delta and cumulative delta, imbalance, absorption, exhaustion, volume profile, replay practice and trade journaling. Students should evaluate data-feed limitations and realistic trading costs before attempting live execution.

Common mistakes

Avoid treating every negative delta bar as a sell, using iceberg or spoofing labels without sufficient evidence, ignoring higher-timeframe location, or interpreting OHLCV-derived estimates as genuine historical bid/ask footprint data.

Frequently asked questions

Can I learn order flow trading from Bangladesh?

Yes. The concepts can be studied online. Live futures order-flow tools may require a paid platform and appropriately licensed exchange data.

Does positive delta mean price must rise?

No. Aggressive buying can be absorbed by passive selling, and price can decline despite positive delta.

Can ordinary candle data produce true footprint charts?

No. Reliable historical bid/ask footprint requires trade-level executions and suitable aggressor-side classification.
